4. SURPRISES AND WHY
======================================================================================================
- "Everything here equals 1": loved by those who stay (37 likes/1k) but only 43.9 % stayed. Its first frame is a
  heart full of small formulas to READ — people swipe before it pays off. Reading-heavy openings lose people.
- "Math sounds part 2" (46 %) vs part 1 (62 %): same idea, 27 s long, weaker first sound. Sequels need a stronger
  first second, not just "more".
- "West Coast beat": weak staying (46 %) but the best subscriber rate (4.25/1k) — music fans who stay, follow.
- Fibonacci part 2: our biggest video, but few shares (0.35/1k) and subs (0.38/1k). It's satisfying to watch,
  not something people send to friends.
