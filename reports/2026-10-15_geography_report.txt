3. WHAT IT MEANS
======================================================================================================
A) The channel is already international and US-led — English titles, USA-friendly upload times are right.
   Keep the evening slot (≈ 22:00 Georgia = 2 pm US East / 8 pm CET).

B) Stayed-to-watch 61 % is good but not yet 65 %+. The best markets for staying are SE Asia + Singapore/HK/AU (65–67 %);
   Japan is the weak spot (48.6 %, no likes) — likely language/caption reading. Short, text-light, visual loops travel best.

C) SHARES ARE THE GAP: 0.4 per 1,000 views. Shares push Shorts to new people.
   → Make "send this to someone" videos: hearts/love (I ♥ U circles, heart equations), "tag a friend who…" puzzles
     (0.999… = 1, the 3 doors, the birthday paradox). Pinned comment: "Send this to the friend who still thinks 0.999… < 1".

D) Comments are low too (0.4 / 1k). The stick-figure puzzles and "comment your answer" quiz endings are the fix
   (e.g. "How many folds to reach the tallest building? 👇").

E) Northern Europe/Japan watch but don't like (DE 3.4, NL 0, SE 0, JP 0 likes per 1k) — no action needed,
   just don't read low likes from these countries as "they didn't enjoy it".

F) Georgia (0.8 %) has much lower staying (32 %) — mostly people who know the channel and your own checks.
   Avoid sending early views from friends/family to new uploads: weak early retention can slow a Short's push.
