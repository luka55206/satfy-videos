   math song 2.9, "fall in love" 2.2, sunflower 2.0 — vs Fibonacci p2 0.34 and perfect tree 0.31.

E) LONG-FORM IS NOT WORKING YET. Hip-hop beat (2:34): 325 views, 12 % watched; ball long-form: 57 views.

   → The 9-min "Equations That Look Like Art" and the Dot films need: a strong thumbnail, a search-phrase title,

   → Don't make more of these.

G) TOO EARLY TO JUDGE: equals one (1.4k), number secrets (1.9k), pumpkin (1.3k in 1 day — it's early for Halloween;
   it should grow toward Oct 31), spin wave (5.7k in 2 days, doing well).
