WHAT IS BETTER
1. For VIEWS: Fibonacci / golden ratio (5k/day median, 116k total from just 3 videos) and escalation ("16 levels").
2. For SUBSCRIBERS: math + familiar music (1.87 per 1k) — π Canon, math song, West Coast beat, Für Elise circle.

3. Best BALANCE (views AND subs): escalation ("16 levels") and math + familiar music.
4. Worst: statistics / plain graphs (130 views/day, 31 % watched) — 7 videos, 4 % of all views. Stop.
5. Illusions/geometry get views but rarely subscribers (0.44) — use them as reach, not as the core.
6. Long-form is barely discovered (43 views/day) but the few viewers subscribe (2.6 per 1k): the audience likes it;
   the packaging (title, thumbnail, related-video links) is what's missing.
