## 4. Settings when posting a Reel
- **Audio:** keep the original audio (it's ours). Do **not** add Instagram music: it can mute the Reel in some countries and hides that the sound is the maths.
- **"Also share to Feed": ON** (so it shows in the grid).
- **Trial reels:** use them for new formats (they're shown to non-followers first, a free A/B test).
- **Label "AI-generated": OFF.** It's code-drawn animation, not generative AI. Same honest answer as on YouTube.
- Add a **topic/keywords** in the caption's first line; Instagram search reads captions.

## 5. Routine
- **1 Reel per day**, the day after it goes up as a Short (the YouTube version gets a head start).
  Best time for USA + Europe: around **22:00 Georgia time** (= 2 pm EST / 8 pm CET), same as your evening Shorts slot.
- **Stories 3–4× per week:** share the new Reel to your story with a poll ("Which sounded better: π or φ?") or the question sticker ("Give me an equation to animate").
- **Reply to every comment in the first hour.** That's the strongest early signal on Reels.
- **Once a week:** a carousel post (e.g. "5 equations that draw art", the thumbnail-style frames) to get saves, which Instagram rewards.
- Do **not** repost the same Reel twice, and don't post a YouTube clip with a visible YouTube UI or watermark. Ours only has the small SatFy mark, which is fine.
