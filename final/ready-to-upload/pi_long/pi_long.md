## Description (paste as is)
Two blocks slide toward a wall with no friction and every collision is counted. A small block against an equal block: 3 collisions. Make the big one 100 times heavier (220 lb vs 2.2 lb): 31. 10,000 times heavier (a 22,000 lb garbage truck): 314. The collisions spell the digits of π, and every 100× heavier block adds one more digit. So we keep making the block heavier: a freight train, an aircraft carrier, the Great Pyramids, all of humanity, Mount Everest, Lake Superior, Pluto, a sixth of the Earth, half the Sun, a third of the Milky Way… and finally a tenth of all the matter in the observable universe.

Counts up to 22,000 lb (10,000 kg) are simulated collision by collision; the bigger ones use the exact formula N = ceil(π/θ) − 1, which matches every simulated count. Based on G. Galperin, "Playing pool with π" (2003).
