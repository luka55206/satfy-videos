## Description
Take a number of miles from the Fibonacci sequence and the next Fibonacci number is the distance in km: 5 mi ≈ 8 km, 8 ≈ 13, 13 ≈ 21. It works because the Fibonacci ratio approaches φ = 1.618, almost exactly 1.609 km per mile.
