accelerating into a sparkling run (fast notes get softer, never louder: per-2 s level -12.8 to -15 dB, no spike).
A warm chord when each count is final. On screen: a centred ticker of the π digits being played (the newest one big
and gold), the counter, "π = 3.14…". The blocks move exactly along the simulated paths; only the playback speed
between hits changes.
v2 ROUND 3 (owner: 314 was too long, monotonous, not satisfying): the 314 digits fly as glowing dots from the impact point onto a RING of 314 slots, heard as 20 harp-like STRUMS of 16 notes, one per beat, the chord changing every 4 strums (Am F C G Am) with a soft wooden beat; at 314 the ring closes into a glowing circle, a warm chord + soft hit, 'π = 3.14…'; then the ring collapses into the loop. (Physically the 314 hits happen in a ~40 ms burst, so any real-time playback is a buzz.)

## Description
Two blocks slide toward a wall with no friction. Count the collisions and you get the digits of π: 3, 31, 314. This time every collision also plays the next digit of π as a note, so you hear π while the blocks count it.
