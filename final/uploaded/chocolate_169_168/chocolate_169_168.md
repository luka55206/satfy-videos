## Description
A 13 by 13 chocolate bar has 169 squares. Cut it into 4 pieces and rebuild it as 8 by 21: only 168. The pieces overlap along the diagonal by exactly one square, because the slopes 3/8 and 5/13 almost match. 5, 8, 13 and 21 are Fibonacci numbers.
