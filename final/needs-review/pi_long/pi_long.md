## Description
Two blocks slide toward a wall with no friction. A 1 kg block and a 1 kg block collide 3 times. Make the big one 100 kg and they collide 31 times, 10,000 kg gives 314… the collisions count the digits of π. This video shows why, then turns the big block into a train, an aircraft carrier, the pyramids, Mount Everest, Pluto, the Sun and the Milky Way, all the way up to a tenth of the matter in the observable universe. Counts up to 10,000 kg are simulated collision by collision; the bigger ones use the exact formula N = ceil(π/θ) − 1. Based on G. Galperin, "Playing pool with π" (2003).
