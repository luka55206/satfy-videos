============================================================
WHY THE VIDEOS FEEL "LOW QUALITY" NOW
============================================================
- Most videos look the same: flat 2-D drawing on a dark background, small labels, nothing grabs the eye in second 1.
- Many are "a process that runs", not a story with a moment you wait for.
  (The Lab gets 1.9M because each video is an EVENT: a reactor melting down, not a graph.)
- The sound is good but not used as the hook (viewers quote it: "third one really said…").
- Viewers swipe away at the fade back to the start (#1 video retention drops in the last 0.5 s).

============================================================
VISUAL UPGRADES FOR EVERY NEW VIDEO
============================================================
1. Depth, not flat: moving camera (zoom / pan / 3-D tilt), bloom glow on bright things,
   motion blur on fast things, particles and sparks on impacts.
2. One HUGE number or object filling the screen in the first second. Labels at least 2x bigger.
3. Impact on every key event: screen shake + flash + punchy sound.
4. Strong end beat: no fade-out. Hard cut to a "?" or a twist so the loop feels like a replay.
5. Distinct, almost voice-like sounds on every event, so people quote them.
