# Niche research — pick the lane before building the car

The factory can render almost anything that code can draw. The niche decides
WHAT, and it is the one decision that is expensive to reverse (a channel's
audience, subs and algorithm profile are all niche-bound). The owner makes the
final call; this skill does the evidence.

## Hard filters — a niche failing ANY of these is out

1. **Code-renderable at quality.** Every frame generated by our pipeline
   (2D/procedural/data-driven animation, simulation, typography, diagrams).
   Needs live filming, a face, gen-AI video, or real-person voice → out.
2. **Survives the inauthentic-content policy.** Each video can carry a
   NAMEABLE difference from the last (a new rule, dataset, question, outcome).
   If episodes can only differ by seed/colour/music → out.
3. **No borrowed IP.** No copyrighted music, characters, footage or brands on
   screen. Data/facts are fine; someone else's clips are not.
4. **Has a loop-able or payoff structure for Shorts** (hook in 2 s, visible
   escalation, payoff) AND can be compiled into 8+ minute long-form (the
   parent's money is ~90% long-form).
5. **Advertiser-friendly** (no gore, shock, medical/financial advice claims).

## Scoring (0-5 each, write the evidence next to every number)

| Axis | What earns a 5 |
|---|---|
| Demand | Several small channels (<50k subs) with Shorts at 100k+ views in the last 90 days — proves breakout from zero is possible |
| Supply gap | Few channels doing it well; top results look cheap or stale |
| Our edge | The pipeline gives us something competitors can't cheaply match (precision, volume, 4K60, deterministic series) |
| Episode depth | 50+ genuinely different episodes are obvious |
| Long-form RPM | Audience/topic with higher-paying advertisers; compilations watchable as background |
| Build cost | First test clip reachable in ≤ 1 day with the existing engine |
| Policy safety | Clearly original, clearly not a re-skin factory |

Total /35. Anything under 22 is not worth a test clip.

## Procedure

1. **Candidates.** Start from the owner's ideas; add 5-10 of your own that pass
   the hard filters. Adjacent-to-parent lanes are cheap (the engine already
   does them) but must not cannibalise the parent channel's audience.
2. **Evidence per candidate** (WebSearch + yt-dlp, never WebFetch on YouTube):
   - 3-5 comparable channels: subs, upload date range, top 5 Shorts views,
     what their hooks/captions/lengths look like.
   - Download 2-3 top references with `yt-dlp --js-runtimes node -f "bv*+ba/b"
     -o "research/reference-videos/ref_<slug>.%(ext)s" --no-playlist <url>`,
     extract frames, READ them: mechanic, escalation, ending, audio, text.
   - Monetisation signals: long-form presence, mid-roll length, sponsor types.
3. **Write `research/niches/<slug>.md`** from `research/niches/_BRIEF_TEMPLATE.md`
   — scores with evidence, 10 episode ideas each with its nameable change, the
   engine changes needed, risks.
4. **Rank and recommend** in chat: top 3, one-line why each, and the single
   cheapest test clip that would prove the leader. Recommend; don't survey.
5. **Owner picks.** Then write `research/niches/CHOSEN.md` (the slug + the
   locked format decisions: look, palette, voice, caption style, length, loop
   style), fill `lib/channel.py` when the channel exists, and re-skin
   `studio.PALETTE` / `layouts.py` / default voice ONCE in lib.
6. Hand off to `make-video` for the first test clip. The first 2 weeks of
   uploads are the real test — schedule a `youtube-analytics` run at day 14
   and let the data re-score the niche.

## Rules

- Evidence over vibes: every score cites a channel, a view count, or a frame.
- Don't recommend a niche because it's big — recommend it because a NEW
  channel demonstrably broke out in it recently.
- Keep the parent's evidence as priors, labelled as such.
- Never write "guaranteed" or promise earnings.
